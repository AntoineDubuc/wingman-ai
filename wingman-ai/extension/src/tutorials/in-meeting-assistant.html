In-Meeting Assistant

Ask questions during a call — grounded in your transcript, persona Knowledge Bases, and (optionally) live web search

1
Switch between Meeting and Assistant

After you start a session, the overlay shows two pills at the top: Meeting (live transcript) and Assistant (chat). Click Assistant to ask the AI a question. Click Meeting to go back to the live transcript. The chat history persists across switches — toggling never resets your conversation.

Independence: The transcript keeps recording in the background even when you're in Assistant mode. Anything said while you were chatting is included in the next question's context.
2
The Assistant empty state

Open the Assistant pill and you'll see the empty state with four suggested prompts. Click any chip to drop it into the input and send. You can also type any question of your own. The context bar at the top shows three toggles: Transcript, Personas (n), and Web Search.

Defaults: Transcript is on. Personas have all your active personas pre-checked. Web Search is off (it costs money per query — you opt in).
3
Ask a general knowledge question

Ask anything you'd ask a chatbot. Turn off all the context toggles and you get plain LLM knowledge — no transcript, no KB. The reply streams in token-by-token. The bottom of each reply shows a small tag indicating which sources were used (here: general knowledge).

4
Ask about the transcript

Leave the Transcript toggle on (default) and ask "what was just said about pricing?" or "summarize the last 5 minutes". The full meeting transcript up to the moment of sending is included in the prompt. The reply tag is transcript, and the answer references things people actually said.

Rolling context: Each question grabs the transcript at send time, not at session start. Follow-up questions auto-incorporate anything new said since your last question.
5
Ask using a Persona Knowledge Base

If you've attached documents to a persona (pricing guides, product specs, objection handling, etc.), check that persona in the Personas dropdown. Asking a question now retrieves matching chunks from those documents and includes them in the prompt. The reply tag shows {Persona} KB — e.g., Architect KB.

You subtract, you don't build: all your active personas are pre-checked. Uncheck the ones you don't want to include in this query. The next query starts with all checked again.
6
Pick which Persona KBs to include

Click the Personas chip to expand the picker. You'll see one checkbox per active persona, each with a colored dot. Check the ones whose KB documents you want included as context for the next question. The count in the chip updates live as you toggle. Collapse the picker by clicking the chip again — your selection is preserved.

Web Search (optional): Toggle Web Search on to include live Brave Search results in the context. You'll need a Brave Search API key in Settings. Each query counts against your daily budget cap (set in Settings → Web Search).
What gets sent where: when you click Send, your question + checked context (transcript / KB chunks / web results) is sent directly from your browser to your configured LLM provider (Gemini, OpenRouter, or Groq) using your own API key. We do not see, store, or proxy any of this. See the privacy policy for full detail.
